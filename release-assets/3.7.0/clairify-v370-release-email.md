Hello,

We're pleased to announce Clairify v3.7.0, our most significant feature release to date. This release is centered on Claire, Clairify's AI assistant, which is now available on mobile and is easier than ever for your organization to deploy, configure, and govern securely.

**Claire Chat on Mobile**
Your team can now work with Claire directly from the Clairify mobile app. Ask questions, use skills, review the sources behind every answer, and approve or decline actions before Claire carries them out.

**Connected Accounts**
Users can connect Slack from their phone and choose exactly which email, calendar, and Slack accounts Claire may work with, and can adjust that access at any time.

**Automations**
A new Automations tab shows the tasks Claire runs on a schedule, when each one ran, and what it found.

**Skills, Files, and Settings**
Administrators can now create and manage their own Claire skills, share reference files with Claire, and choose which categories of email Claire receives, all from the admin console.

**Claire Tools**
Administrators can extend what Claire can do from a central tools catalog, using built-in tools such as web search, custom tools that connect to your own business systems, or your own MCP server. Every tool is versioned, verifiable, and runs in a sandbox.

**Your Choice of AI Engine**
Run Claire on Clairify's built-in AI engine with no setup required, or bring your own OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, or self-hosted model. A backup model can be configured so Claire keeps working if a provider has an outage, and usage and cost are visible by provider.

**Security, Zero Trust, and AI Safety**
Claire acts only as the signed-in user, with only the access that user grants, and never sees passwords or API keys. Each person's accounts are isolated, no one at Clairify can view your data through Claire, incoming email is screened for hidden instructions aimed at AI, sensitive actions require your approval, and every connection is encrypted and verified.

**Simpler Instance Management**
A new health dashboard and faster management screens make it straightforward to keep every Claire instance running smoothly across your organization.

**Automatic Email Signatures**
Emails sent through Clairify now include your Gmail or Outlook signature automatically, and signatures can be reviewed or edited at any time.

**30 Days of Inbox History**
Customers on paid plans can now bring in the last 30 days of email, so Claire has meaningful context from day one.

**Additional Improvements**
Notifications now identify the email they relate to, swipe actions are clearer, read status syncs more frequently, invitation emails have a refreshed design, and administrators can adjust feature limits for individual users.

This release also resolves several smaller issues, including draft replies that regenerated each time they were reopened and an onboarding badge that displayed an incorrect count.

As always, we'd love your feedback. Send questions, comments, and feature requests to support@clairify.ai.

Best,

The Clairify Team
