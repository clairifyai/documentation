# Pairing Prompts and Categories

To customize the way an email looks you need to pair its category with a summarization prompt. There are two types of summarization prompts, one we provide (system), or one you make yourself (custom).

If you create a custom category, you would have selected a summarization prompt at that time. You would typically follow the instructions below to *change* the system prompt associated to a category. 

To change the prompt associated to a category:

1. Open the navigation panel.
1. Select **Content Management > Emails**.
1. Click the **Mappings** tab.
1. Find the tile for your category.
1. Click **Edit**.
