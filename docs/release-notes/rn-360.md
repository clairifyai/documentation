### Chat with Your Calendar

Claire now speaks Google Calendar. Connect your calendar and ask Claire to check your availability, or create, update, and delete events — all from the chat interface, no need to open Calendar separately.

### Move a Secondary Account to Its Own Account

You can now separate a secondary email from your primary Clairify account — either promoting it to become your new primary, or spinning it off as a fully independent account, with all of its data, settings, and history carried over. This is available from the app and the web admin console, and org admins can do it on a member's behalf when offboarding them.

### Organization Hierarchy for MSPs

MSPs can now model their customers as a true hierarchy — regions, departments, and sub-teams nested under one customer relationship — with settings and licensing that cascade down the tree. A few things that come with it:

- Org admins can invite new team members by email, with the invitee's account ready to go the moment they log in.
- Admins get full control over any sub-organization they manage, while a handful of sensitive actions on their own top-level organization — like granting free licensing — stay reserved for Clairify staff.
- Adding a member can no longer push an organization past its licensed seat count.

### Provisioning New Team Members

Admins can now provision a teammate's account — email, license, and all — before that person has ever logged in, so it's ready to go the moment they do. New team members automatically get a welcome email with a download link and setup instructions as soon as they're provisioned.

### Accounts Center

The mobile app's drawer has been redesigned into a single Accounts Center, consolidating your profile, settings, and help & info into one place instead of spreading them across separate screens.

### Remember My Email

The login screen now shows which email address you last signed in with, making it easier to pick the right one and avoid accidentally creating a duplicate account or hitting a sign-in error.

### App Update Reminders

The app now checks for newer releases and shows a friendly reminder if you're behind — tap through to update, or dismiss and we'll check back tomorrow. You can also see your current app version and the latest available version at a glance.
