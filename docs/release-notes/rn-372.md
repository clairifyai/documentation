## Claire

Clairify 3.7.2 gives administrators and MSPs a clearer view of what Claire's tools do and faster ways to set Claire up for their teams. Each tool now shows which outside services it connects to and which of its actions need a person's approval, and Claire now asks before it posts in Slack. Admins can also set up a new member's tools and skills in one guided step and roll out tools, skills, and scheduled jobs to many instances at once.

### See What Each Tool Does

MSPs and organization admins can now review how every tool in the tool library behaves before they let Claire use it.

- **Third-party services:** Each tool lists the outside services it connects to, with the host shown up front and the full address available. Tools that connect to no outside service say so. The tool list also shows these services, so you can scan the library without opening each tool.
- **Human approval:** Each tool shows whether it waits for a person's approval before it runs, and how risky the action is, such as changing data, sending invitations, deleting data, or sending data. Tools that run without approval are clearly marked.
- **Per-action detail:** For tools that offer several actions, such as the Slack workspace tools, the tool view shows which actions need approval.
- **Available to your admins:** Organization admins can see this information for every tool their organization can use, without contacting Clairify.

### Approval Before Claire Acts in Slack

Claire now asks for approval before it posts a message, replies to a thread, or adds a reaction in Slack. Slack actions use the same approval card, risk levels, and "allow for this chat" option as Claire's other tools, and approval rules are now checked consistently for every tool in the catalog.

### Set Up Members and Roll Out Tools Faster

Admins can now set up Claire for their team in far fewer steps.

- **Set up member wizard:** When you add a member to a Claire instance, a guided setup opens. Start from scratch or copy another member's setup, then pick tools and the skills they unlock. Items that can't be selected show why. Review every addition and removal before you apply them, follow the progress, and retry anything that fails. You can skip the setup and come back to it at any time.
- **Members table:** A new Setup column, a "Needs setup" filter, and a sidebar count show which members still need to be set up. You can copy one member's setup to several members at once.
- **Roll out from the catalog:** Roll out a tool, skill, or scheduled job to many instances in one flow. Choose who can see it and whether to install it for every eligible member, review the change, then see the result for each instance. Rolling out tools is limited to superadmins.

## Enhancements

### Pinned Channels

The mobile Channels screen now shows only your pinned channels, so the features you use most are front and center.

- **Pin what matters:** Open **All channels** to pin or unpin any channel, and drag pinned channels to reorder them. Swipe left on a pinned channel to unpin it.
- **Synced across devices:** Your pins are saved to your account and stay the same after a restart and on your other devices.
- **Sensible defaults:** New users start with Instack, Mailroom, Claire, and Integrations pinned. Administrators can change these default pins in System Settings. A change applies to everyone who hasn't set their own pins yet.
- **Claire by name:** The Assistant channel is now called **Claire**.

### Process Older Emails

After you clear your stack, Clairify can now keep working through older emails still sitting in your Inbox. When you reach "All caught up!", tap **Process 10 older emails** to pull the next batch of older Inbox emails into your stack, read or unread. They are summarized and sorted like any new email. This works for primary and secondary accounts. This feature depends on your plan.

### Personal Data Masked Before Summarization

Personal details in your emails, such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, account numbers, and street addresses, are now masked before the email is sent to the AI model for summarization. The original details are restored in your summary, so it reads normally, and they are never logged or stored by the masking step.

### Bug Fixes

- **Fixed:** Swiping right on an email you've already read now works instead of showing an error.
- **Fixed:** Saved API keys and secrets are now masked to a short, consistent length, display on one line, and never reveal most of a short key.
- **Fixed:** The confirmation email sent after reporting a bug by shaking your phone no longer includes internal diagnostic logs.
- **Fixed:** In the mobile app, the "Use personalization" setting can no longer be changed when your organization has turned personalization off.
- **Fixed:** Two admins changing who can see a skill at the same time no longer remove that skill from a member's assistant.
