# Member Offboarding

When someone leaves your organization, you can remove their access to Claire and delete their Claire data in one process.

## What happens when you remove a member

- **Immediate access removal:** Access is cut off within minutes, whether the member is removed through an organization change or their account is deleted. This includes their connected credentials, tools, and running services.
- **Retention period:** A removed member's data is kept for a retention period (30 days by default) before it is permanently deleted. If the member rejoins during the retention period, they keep all of their data.
- **Deleted accounts:** A deleted account's data is purged immediately, with no retention period.
- **Protection against reuse:** A new account created with a deleted account's email address can't see or inherit any of the old account's data.

!!! warning
    The admin console warns you before you remove members or move an instance to another organization. Read the warning before you confirm.

## Managing removed members

- **Archived tab:** See removed members and the date each one's data is scheduled for deletion. From here you can purge a member's data early.
- **Retention settings:** In **System Settings**, set the retention period anywhere from 1 to 3,650 days, and turn automatic purging on or off.

!!! note
    Every purge is recorded in the audit log.
